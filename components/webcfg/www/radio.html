Read the frequency, mode and the rest, as JSON:

Set the frequency (Hz, or MHz with a point) and anything else on this page:

Another radio, by its place in the list from 0 — the knob restarts into it:
POST /api/radios/switch with to=1

Another receiver, by its place in the list from 0, at once — even while the one in use is down: receiver=1

Another band, by its place in the receiver's own list from 0 (owrx.bands in the JSON): band=3 — it moves everyone listening to the receiver, and is refused within 11 s of the last change, which OpenWebRX+ bans a listener for.

Another band, by its place in the site's own list from 0 (wsdr.bands in the JSON: its bands, or a one-band site's band plan): band=3 — the dial onto it, moving nobody else.

Also mode, filter (1–3), agc, gain (the noise filter: 0 off, 1 WDSP, 2 LMS, 3 spectral), rfgain and power (0–100), squelch (0–100 of the S-meter's scale, 0 open), tuner (on/off), rx (main/sub), ant and txant (by place in the radio's list, from 1), rit (Hz), and sdr (local, or a web SDR by its place in the list, from 0) and balance (−100 the radio alone, 0 the radio left and the SDR right, 100 the SDR alone), and sdr, the right ear (off, or a receiver by its place in the list, from 0 — not the one in use) and balance (−100 the left ear alone, 0 left and right, 100 the right alone). Both need this page's login — http://user:password@… — and nothing here transmits.
