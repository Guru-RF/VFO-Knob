Design C wins: draw each meter as one plain LVGL object that paints its own arcs. On the models it brings the call VU from the measured 53-64% of core 1 down to about 2-4%, and the meters look the same as now. Nothing was edited, built or flashed. All cost figures below come from models; only the 53-64% was measured on the device. ui.c line numbers are from the 4,727-line copy saved at 01:30. The file was being edited while I read it, so look every name up again before editing.

WHY THE VU IS EXPENSIVE (checked against LVGL 9.3.0, esp_lvgl_port 2.9.0 and ui.c)

1. Peak LEDs repaint the whole arc several times a second.
   - lv_arc's angle setters redraw the whole 344x344 object (91% of the screen) when the span wraps (lv_arc.c:107, 130, 160, 182).
   - led_set (ui.c:501-519) collapses the old zone's LED to (0,0) at ui.c:515. That wraps whenever the LED's start angle is below 177 degrees, which is every zone change.
   - Any LED move of more than 3 degrees toward the start of its half also wraps. The mirrored mic half does this on almost every rising step (over about 1.9 dB).
2. Every visible arc costs time under every redraw inside its circle.
   - Each arc draw covers its whole circle box (lv_draw_arc.c:60-65). Each row of box ∩ dirty area gets a memset, three masks and a blend (lv_draw_sw_arc.c:64, 186-222).
   - The inner-radius mask zeroes the ring's hole but never returns "transparent" (lv_draw_sw_mask.c:860-889). Labels in the middle therefore pay for every arc.
   - A call shows 35 such arcs: s_ring, plus two times (track + 8 zones + 8 LEDs).
3. s_ring (ui.c:2745-2753) is a 356 px ring in the background colour, invisible but never hidden. Its whole box is redrawn on every refresh.

Mechanisms each design relies on:
- A: children and invalidations are clipped to the parent (lv_refr.c:134-145, lv_obj_pos.c:894-913). Holds.
- C: a widget may narrow layer->_clip_area before adding draw tasks. lv_draw.h:86-94 documents it, lv_draw.c:109 copies it into each task, and lv_chart, lv_roller and lv_dropdown do it. Holds.
- B: an A8 image is drawn as a masked fill in the recolour (lv_draw_sw_img.c:237-249). Holds.

LVGL limits:
- A 33rd dirty area makes LVGL repaint the whole screen (lv_refr.c:323).
- LVGL busy-waits while a strip is sent (lv_refr.c:1429), and that wait counts as taskLVGL time.
- Invalidating from inside a draw callback trips an assert (lv_refr.c:277).

Three problems none of the designs mentioned:
- **Dotted labels redraw every tick.** set_text (ui.c:3564) compares against lv_label_get_text(). LV_LABEL_LONG_DOT writes the dots into the stored text (lv_label.c:1223-1237), so the comparison always fails. Any dotted label (s_hs_name at 3999, s_ptt_lbl at 3973, a radio-face s_mem_big that overflows) is set and redrawn at 20 Hz.
- **The scrolling caller name costs about 4-5%.** s_mem_big scrolls in a loop on the phone (ui.c:3006). Each 1 px step at 40 px/s redraws its 300x34 box (lv_label.c:33, 1241-1246). That stays true even with cheap meters, and it now matters because the caller's name replaces the favourite during a call.
- **The ringing slab pulls in all 35 arcs.** The breathing ANSWER slab (y 248-359) sits inside all 35 arc boxes, so each animation step runs all of them.

SCORES (1-5, higher is better)

| Design | Cost | Look | Risk | Effort | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A, clip cells | 3.5 | 4.5 | 4 | 4.5 | 16.5 |
| B, A8 sprites | 5 | 2.5 | 2.5 | 3 | 13 |
| C, band objects | 4.5 | 5 | 4 | 3.5 | 17 |

- **A:**
  - Cost: its model gives 3.6-4.7%, pessimistic 8-13%. Each touched cell still has 2 styled arcs. The SDR line and power bar stay full-size arcs, and a sweep across quadrants still dirties their whole circle (lv_draw_arc.c:162-167).
  - Look: the track splits into 8 pieces, leaving 1-pixel seams. The next zone's track can cover the LED's last degree.
  - Risk and effort: adds 16 objects (about 3 kB of LVGL heap); about 150 lines.
- **B:**
  - Cost: its model gives 1.2-1.3%, or 2.6-2.8% when scaled the way the current layout needs to match the measurement.
  - Look: the bar end moves in 1.6-degree (1 dB) steps instead of 1 degree. Bar and LED ends get a hard, non-antialiased edge. The LED becomes 2 cells that can show two colours.
  - Risk and effort: a new rasteriser, 13-33 kB of sprites in PSRAM, and about 300 lines removed across all 8 firmwares; 1-2 days.
- **C:**
  - Cost: its model gives 1.9-4.0% at full call activity, 7.4% in a synthetic worst case.
  - Look: the same LVGL arc rasteriser, settings and drawing order, so the look is unchanged. The bar end can differ by up to 1 degree. Mic and SWR track seams disappear.
  - Risk: it writes layer->_clip_area, which is internal but documented as settable, on LVGL pinned at 9.3.0. It frees about 15-20 kB of LVGL heap.

WINNER: C, with these taken from the others

- From all three: hide s_ring whenever its colour would be the background.
- From A: ship in steps, check the cells by tinting them, and stop the caller-name scroll during calls.
- From B: write nothing per tick except invalidating the sectors that changed.
- From this review:
  - merge the LED's old and new blocks when they touch;
  - cap dirty areas per meter per update;
  - optionally make release() time-based;
  - fix set_text for dotted labels;
  - optionally skip meter writes while the panel is blanked.

PLAN

1. Objects and geometry

All angles are absolute LVGL degrees (0 = 3 o'clock, clockwise). Centre is (180,180), radius r is the outer edge, width w. "Fill frame" means degrees counted from the end the meter fills from. Each object's box is its sector box plus 2 px.

| Phone VU, our mic (s_vu[1]) | 273 (AUD_ROT) | 97 | 170 / 12 | yes | same | about (185,8)-(352,214) | 13 |

| s_sdr_band (VFO_HAS_SDR) | 170 | 200 | 176 / 4 | no | one zone [0,200] in C_SDR, LED SDR_HEX | (not computed) | 28 |

- Notches and ticks stay as lv_line objects in the existing groups: the phone's marks, s_rx_ticks and s_tx_ticks. Those groups stay lifted above the bands (ui.c:3719, 2946-2947).

2. band_t and helpers

Put them after led_show (ui.c:521-527) so build() and the VU can use them. Use EXT_RAM_BSS_ATTR for the radio-face bands.

    typedef struct { int16_t x1, y1, x2, y2; } box16_t;
    typedef struct {
        lv_obj_t *obj; int16_t rot, span, r, w; bool mirror; uint8_t nz, ncell;
        int16_t a0[8], a1[8]; lv_color_t zc[8], track;
        box16_t zbox[8], cell[28], ledbox;
        int16_t bar, led; int8_t led_z;      /* fill-frame degrees; led = -1: out */
    } band_t;

- **band_abs(b, a):** mirror ? rot+span−a : rot+a.
- **band_sector(b, lo, hi, &box):**
  - convert fill-frame to absolute [A,B];
  - corners at radius r+1 and r−w−1, using lv_trigo_cos/sin >> LV_TRIGO_SHIFT;
  - add r+1 at every multiple of 90 inside [A,B];
  - pad 2 px and clamp to the object's box.
- **band_build(b, rot, span, r, w, mirror, nz, a0[], a1[], rgb[]):**
  - lv_obj_create(s_scr), lv_obj_remove_style_all;
  - remove CLICKABLE and SCROLLABLE (taps stay coordinate-based, ui.c:2238-2265);
  - set position and size from band_sector(0, span);
  - lv_obj_add_event_cb(obj, band_draw_cb, LV_EVENT_DRAW_MAIN, b);
  - compute zbox[z] and the cells; track = C_SUBTLE; bar = 0; led = −1.
- **band_cells:**
  - walk the box in 16-row strips;
  - in each row, test only pixels inside the ring between r−w−1.5 and r+1.5, found with sqrtf;
  - keep those whose centre angle (atan2f) is within [rot−1, rot+span+1];
  - per strip, record the x extent separately left and right of x=180, plus 1 px.
  - Run once at boot; well under 2 ms per band.
- **band_draw_cb:**

      const lv_area_t clip0 = layer->_clip_area;
      d: lv_draw_arc_dsc_init; center (180,180); radius r; width w; rounded 0
      for each cell meeting clip0:
          layer->_clip_area = cell ∩ clip0;  arc(track, 0, span)
          for z while a0[z] < bar:  if (cell ∩ clip0 ∩ zbox[z]) arc(zc[z], a0[z], min(bar, a1[z]))
          if led >= 0 and (cell ∩ clip0 ∩ ledbox): arc(zc[led_z], led, led + LED_DEG)
      layer->_clip_area = clip0;

  - arc() converts to absolute degrees and calls lv_draw_arc.
  - The cells don't overlap, so every pixel gets track, then zones, then LED, as today.
  - Never invalidate inside this callback.
- **band_set(b, bar_f, pk_f, pk_z):**
  - bar = clamp((int)(bar_f*span), 0, span);
  - led = (pk_f > 0 && pk_z >= 0) ? clamp((int)(pk_f*span + 0.5f) − LED_DEG, 0, span − LED_DEG) : −1. This is led_set's rule, and it also holds for mirrored meters.
  - If the bar changed, call band_inv(min, max).
  - If the LED moved or changed zone: invalidate the union of old and new blocks when they overlap or touch, otherwise each block on its own. Then update ledbox.
  - If nothing changed, write nothing.
- **band_inv:** split the range into pieces of at least 16 degrees, at most 6 pieces, and call lv_obj_invalidate_area for each. That gives at most 8 areas per meter per tick: phone at most 18 in total, S-meter plus SDR line at most 16.
- **band_show(b, on):** toggle HIDDEN only on change. A hidden band invalidates nothing; showing it repaints its box once.

3. Update rate and throttling

- ui_task stays the only writer, at 20 Hz (app_main.c:879-884).
- Integer degrees mean an unchanged meter costs nothing.
- Ballistics are unchanged:
  - release() 35% per tick;
  - peak_hold: hold 1 s, then fall at 30 dB/s (VU, S-meter, SDR), 20 dB/s (mic), 0.5×full-scale/s (power), 1.0/s (SWR).
- Optional: make release time-based. Compute s_rel_k = 1 − expf(−0.008616f × clamp(dt_ms, 10, 250)) once per ui_update right after ui.c:4008, and use it in release() (ui.c:452-456). A skipped tick then no longer changes the fall.
- Optional: skip meter writes while s_level == LVL_BLANK. s_level is declared at ui.c:4688, after ui_update, so it needs a forward declaration. The next band_set after waking redraws the difference correctly.
- **Meters fed by radio and SDR data streams don't change the drawing cost.** TCI, VITA-49, CI-V, Kiwi and UberSDR values are parsed on the clients' own tasks; the face samples them once per 50 ms tick. So the meters can stay on everywhere.

8. Pitfalls

- Restore layer->_clip_area before returning from the draw callback.
- The band object's box must include the anti-aliased edges (r+1, r−w−1, plus 2 px); LVGL clips each object to its own box.
- Cells must not overlap and must include every anti-aliased pixel.
- Zone a0/a1 must be computed exactly as today (truncation, and the mic's +0.5).
- Create bands where their arcs were; s_ring before the SDR band; notch groups lifted last.
- In transmit, a power-range change can add about 18 areas. Going past 32 forces one full repaint (about 15-30 ms), which is rare and acceptable.
- LVGL's circle-mask cache holds 4 entries and is emptied every refresh. If circ_calc_aa4 shows up in a profile, raise CONFIG_LV_DRAW_SW_CIRCLE_CACHE_SIZE.
- Angles are whole degrees (LV_USE_FLOAT is off).
- Changing the background colour for transmit still repaints the whole screen once per PTT edge.
